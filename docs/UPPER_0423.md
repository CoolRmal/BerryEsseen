# A second, sharper upper bound: `berryEsseenConstant ≤ 0.423`

```lean
theorem BerryEsseen.Upper0423.berryEsseenConstant_le_0423 :
    BerryEsseen.berryEsseenConstant ≤ 0.423
```

This is proved in `BerryEsseen/Upper0423/Main.lean`, independently of (and without
modifying) the `0.4688` development. Build and check it with

```
lake build BerryEsseen.Upper0423.Main
```

The default library target does not import `Upper0423`, so `lake build BerryEsseen` and the
existing CI and comparator setup are unaffected.

**Axioms.** `#print axioms` reports `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` and the
auxiliary axioms generated by `native_decide` (one per finite certificate, 579 in total).
There is no `sorry` and no custom axiom. Unlike the `0.4688` proof, this proof therefore
trusts Lean's compiler for the finite checks. The mathematical source is the
computer-assisted proof `research/PROOF_0423.md`; its external input (Shevtsova's bound for
large third moments) is **not** used — the large-moment region is proved directly.

## Provenance and attribution

The analytic framework of this proof is not new here. The research notes
`research/imaginary-envelope.md` ("Source alignment and attribution") and
`research/recovery/RECOVERY.md` attribute the following ingredients to
[haonan-xiao/iid-berry-esseen, commit `f00d781c`](https://github.com/haonan-xiao/iid-berry-esseen/tree/f00d781c8c42908bf4aef2739129442a0919fa38),
which proves a `0.4395` bound for the same constant:

* the shared moment parameters, the radial rectangle and the disk/strip discussion, the shared
  convex profile, the moment region, and the Prawitz normalization convention
  (their `proof-guide.md`, sections 2–3 and 5–6);
* the two-point sine-circle mechanism (their `SineRemainder.lean`), formalized here in
  `Analytic/SineCircle.lean`;
* the stop-loss identity for `E|X − X'|³` and the resulting bounds on the symmetrization
  excess `η` (their `ThirdMomentRatio.lean`), here `Analytic/StopLoss.lean` and
  `Analytic/Moments.lean`;
* the separate radial estimates for the real and imaginary parts (their
  `TwoPointComparison.lean`), here `Analytic/Radial.lean`;
* the cosine constant `κ = max_{v>0} (cos v − 1 + v²/2)/v³`, bracketed there as
  `0.09916191350 < κ < 0.09916191353`; this development uses the coarser rational majorant
  `49581/500000` and the same convex profile `1/2 − κv` continued by `(1 − cos v)/v²`
  (`Analytic/CosineProfile.lean`).

Every one of these lemmas is proved from scratch in Lean here and checked by the kernel, but
the mathematical ingredients and the overall approach are theirs. None of their certificates
or numerical budgets are used: the research notes record that their certificates and their
`56/45` domain cutoff are specific to the `0.4395` theorem. No claim is made here about the
correctness of that theorem.

The ingredients the research notes claim as additions are the anchored imaginary envelope,
the signed real interval, the cosine lower bound, the geometric-sum comparison, and — in this
Lean development — the uniform-in-`n` certificate that removes the published Shevtsova input
used by the research proof.

## Case split

Write `β = E|X|³`, `η` for the symmetrization excess, and `ℓ = β/√n`.

| Region | Lean theorem | Method |
|---|---|---|
| `n = 1` | `normalizedError_one_le` (`SingleSummand.lean`) | rational polynomial majorants, no computation |
| `ℓ ≥ 13003/10000` | `normalizedError_large_ell` (`LargeEll.lean`) | Cantelli: `|F − Φ| ≤ 11/20` |
| `2 ≤ n ≤ 255`, `ℓ < 13003/10000` | `normalizedError_moderate_le` (`Covers/All.lean`) | per-`n` covers of the moment parameters, checked by `native_decide` |
| `n ≥ 256`, `ℓ ≤ 13003/10000` | `normalizedError_uniform` (`Uniform/Data/All.lean`) | one certificate uniform in `n` and `β` |

### Per-sample-size covers (`2 ≤ n ≤ 255`)

For fixed `n`, the Prawitz smoothing inequality with cutoff `T = c√n` is bounded by an
interval/Taylor-jet engine (`Native/Interval.lean`, `Native/Majorant.lean`) whose soundness is
proved in `Native/*Sound.lean` from the characteristic-function estimates in `Analytic/`
(`ChfFactsProof.lean`). A cover is a split tree of boxes in `(δ, w)`, `β = 1 + δ`,
`η = 1 − δw` (for `β ≤ 2`), or directly in `(β, η)` (for `β ≥ 2`), with the smoothing
parameters and frequency panels of every leaf.

* `Small/Data/Cover{2..63}.lean`: the original research covers, `β ≤ 712569/454000`;
* `Covers/Data/DW{n}.lean`, `Covers/Data/BE{n}.lean`: the remaining boxes up to
  `β ≤ 1.3003·√n` (507 files, about 12,000 leaves).

### The uniform certificate (`n ≥ 256`)

With `a = β + η`, `L = a/√n`, `h = 1/√n` and cutoff `T = 2π/L`, every term of the Prawitz bound
normalized by `√n/β` is at most (`Uniform/Reduction.lean`)

`(a/β)·(highF L + normalF L + tailF L)/L + (Γ/β)·lowG(L, h) + (h/β)·lowK(L, h)`,

where the five functions are one-dimensional integrals depending only on `L` and `h`
(`Uniform/Defs.lean`). The moment constraints give `(Γ/β)² ≤ 2(1 − η/β)` and cap `η/β` in
terms of `L`. Verified interval checkers bound the integrals on cells `[L₁, L₂]`
(`Uniform/HighTail.lean`, `NormalBound.lean`, `LowBound.lean`), and `Uniform/Budget.lean`
combines them. 281 geometric cells cover `L ∈ (1/100, 2.6006]`, plus an analytic first cell;
the largest certified value is about `0.4150`.

## Reproducing the certificates

The Lean data files are generated by the scripts in `scripts/upper_0423/`:

* `run_covers.sh` (with `make_jobs.py`, `cover_gen.py`): searches the covers using the
  research producer `research/native_cover.py` and its C++ engine (set `BERRY_RESEARCH` if
  the research directory is not next to this repository);
* `gen_covers.py`, `gen_all.py`: write `Covers/Data/*.lean` and `Covers/All.lean`;
* `gen_small_cover.py`, `gen_small_data.py`: write `Small/Data/*.lean` from the research covers;
* `gen_uniform.py 256 102/100 35`: writes `Uniform/Data/*.lean`.

The search itself is untrusted; every accepted bound is recomputed by the Lean checkers.
